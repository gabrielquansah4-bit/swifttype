Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about SwiftType.

What services does SwiftType provide?

SwiftType provides document typing, data entry, CV and resume formatting, reports and assignments, basic transcription and document formatting.

How do I request a quotation?

Use the Get a Quote page and provide the details of your project. We will review the requirements and respond accordingly.

How is pricing calculated?

Pricing depends on the type, size and complexity of the project. We provide quotations based on the requirements rather than applying one price to every project.

Can you type handwritten documents?

Yes, suitable handwritten documents can be typed. The clarity and quality of the source material may affect the amount of work required.

Can you format CVs and resumes?

Yes. SwiftType provides professional CV and resume formatting for job seekers and professionals.

Do you provide transcription?

Yes. We provide basic transcription of clear audio recordings into organized written documents.

How do I contact SwiftType?

You can contact us through email, WhatsApp or the contact form on this website.

Do you handle documents confidentially?

We understand that documents may contain private or sensitive information and aim to handle client materials responsibly and professionally.
