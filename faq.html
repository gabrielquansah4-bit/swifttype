Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers about SwiftType services, pricing and requests.

What services does SwiftType provide?

SwiftType provides document typing, data entry, CV formatting, reports and assignments, basic transcription and document formatting.

How do I request a quote?

You can use the Request a Quote page on the website or contact SwiftType directly through WhatsApp.

How do I send my document?

After submitting your request, you can send your document through WhatsApp or email so we can review the work required.

How long does the work take?

Turnaround depends on the size, complexity and deadline of the task. We confirm the expected turnaround after reviewing your request.

Do you work with businesses and organizations?

Yes. SwiftType can provide typing, data entry and document support for businesses, schools, organizations and individuals.

Do you accept urgent requests?

Urgent requests may be possible depending on workload and the required deadline. Contact us as early as possible.

How much does typing cost?

Standard typing starts from GH₵5 per page, while handwritten typing starts from GH₵8 per page. Final pricing depends on the task.

How much does CV formatting cost?

CV formatting starts from GH₵30 per CV.

How do I pay?

Payment arrangements are discussed and confirmed when your request and quotation are agreed.

Will my documents be treated confidentially?

SwiftType aims to handle client documents responsibly and maintain appropriate confidentiality when working on client materials.
